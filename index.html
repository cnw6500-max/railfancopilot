Frequently Asked Questions

What is RailFan Copilot?

RailFan Copilot is an iOS app designed to help railroad enthusiasts track trains, explore routes, and enhance their railfanning experience.

How do I restore my Pro subscription?

Open the app, go to Settings → Subscription, and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID used to purchase the subscription.

My subscription isn't working — what should I do?

  • Sign out of your Apple ID and sign back in.
  • Go to Settings → Subscription → Restore Purchases.
  • If the issue persists, contact us using the email below.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your Apple ID. Go to Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone or iPad to cancel at any time.
